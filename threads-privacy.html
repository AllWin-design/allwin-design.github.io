Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy applies to the Meta app "自動發文系統" (the "App"), a personal tool used by a single Threads/Instagram account that shares basketball game analysis.

What the App does

  • Publishes posts, images and videos to the owner's own Threads and Instagram accounts.
  • Uses Threads keyword search to find recent public posts discussing NBA/WNBA games, and sends a short list (post text and link) privately to the account owner. The owner reads the list and decides manually whether to reply.

What the App does not do

  • It does not reply, like, follow, repost or send messages automatically.
  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties.
  • It does not collect data from users of any other app or website.

Data we keep

Only the IDs of public posts already shown to the owner are stored, to avoid showing the same post twice. These IDs are deleted automatically after 7 days. Post text is not stored.

Data deletion

To request deletion of any data related to you, contact us at the email below. We will delete it within 7 days. Because stored post IDs expire automatically after 7 days, no action is usually needed.
